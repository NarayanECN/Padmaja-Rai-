Dr. Padmaja Rai (Ph.D.)

Plant Physiologist & Biotechnologist

Ph.D. in Biotechnology (2024), MNNIT Allahabad
Banaras Hindu University (BHU)

I am an agricultural plant biotechnologist specializing in abiotic stress management, plant biochemistry, and sustainable nano-agriculture. My core research unravels nanomaterial-plant interactions, the biochemical roles of silicon and signaling molecules like Nitric Oxide and Hydrogen Sulfide, and gene expression profiling to mitigate environmental vulnerabilities. Additionally, I integrate Machine Learning and Bioinformatics to advance molecular biology research.

Research Framework & Expertise

Nano-Agriculture

Synthesis, uptake, and translocation dynamics of targeting nanoparticles (e.g., ZnO, AgNPs, Nanoiron, CuO NPs) inside complex plant systems to improve crop safety.

Abiotic Stress Signalling

Decoding the ascorbate-glutathione cycle and cascades involving Nitric Oxide (NO), Hydrogen Sulfide, and Ethylene to neutralize toxic Chromium, Cadmium, and Arsenate stresses.

Bioinformatics & ML

Applying computational machine learning tools (Python, C++), bioinformatics, and functional genomics to profile stress-responsive gene variants and transcriptomic shifts.

Gene Expression & Hormones

Utilizing qRT-PCR molecular profiling to analyze genes (e.g., PCR1) and evaluating cross-talk mechanisms of elemental silicon and auxin (IAA) in root architecture.
