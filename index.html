Dr. Padmaja Rai

Research Associate-I

Department of Genetics and Plant Breeding
Institute of Agricultural Sciences, Banaras Hindu University (BHU)

I am a Plant Biotechnologist specializing in agricultural nanotechnology, plant physiology, and abiotic stress management. My work focuses on leveraging nano-scale materials and physiological interventions (such as Silicon applications) to mitigate environmental toxicities and enhance structural crop resilience. I completed my Ph.D. in Biotechnology from MNNIT Allahabad.

Research Framework

Nano-Agriculture

Synthesis, characterization, and deployment of nanoparticles to enhance crop yield and defense systems safely.

Abiotic Stress

Evaluating plant morpho-physiological and biochemical profiles under heavy metal toxicity and drought pressures.

Silicon Nutrition

Investigating mechanisms of silicon-mediated elemental cross-talk to counter environmental stresses in higher plants.

Gene Expression

Utilizing molecular profiling to monitor stress-responsive genes and transcriptomic shifts in crop cultivars.
